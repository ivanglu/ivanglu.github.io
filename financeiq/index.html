How FinanceIQ works

  • Data. Every figure comes from a company's annual report (Form 10-K), downloaded from the SEC's public EDGAR API. Numbers are shown as first reported, so they match the linked filing. Data last refreshed —.
  • Fiscal years. Companies end their financial year in different months, so "FY2026" for one company may cover different months than for another.
  • Formulas. Net margin = net income ÷ revenue. Free cash flow = operating cash flow − capital spending. Debt = short- and long-term borrowings (leases excluded). Cash includes short-term investments. Growth per year is the compound annual rate over five years.
  • Not investment advice. This is a student project for learning how to read financial statements.
