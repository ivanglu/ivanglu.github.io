Projects

  • Flagship project Finance · Data analytics JavaScript · Python · SEC EDGAR

    FinanceIQ

    A stock research tool that reads ten years of annual and quarterly reports for 38 public companies straight from the SEC, and turns them into ratings, screens and valuations that show their working.

    • Stock ratings: each company graded on fundamentals, earnings momentum and financial quality, with every measure shown.
    • Screener and search: sort, filter, compare up to four companies, and look any of them up by ticker or name.
    • DCF valuation simulator: scenarios, sensitivity table and reverse DCF.
    • Tested: every formula has automated tests, and stored figures are checked against the filings.
